## 1.5 Why imported skincare costs what it does in Pakistan

- **Primary keyword:** why is imported skincare so expensive in Pakistan
- **Secondary:** customs duty on cosmetics in Pakistan · imported cosmetics price increase Pakistan · skincare price difference UAE Pakistan · imported skincare duty 2026
- **Intent:** Informational, high trust-building value, strong link-earning potential. Not a direct money page — it feeds the cluster.
- **What ranking pages appear to cover:** news coverage only, and none of it skincare-specific. ProPakistani, Samaa, ARY News and Times of Oman covered duty hikes; one summary cites SRO.928(I)/2024 imposing a 55% regulatory duty on imported cosmetics from July 2024, with a stacked structure (customs duty 20–35%, additional customs duty 2–7%, regulatory duty 10–50%, FED 10%, sales tax 18%) applied to cost-and-freight value. Reports from mid-2025 suggested reductions in the 2025-26 budget, by 2–5 percentage points. **No retailer connects any of this to shelf prices.**
- **The gap you fill:** this is the honest, structural answer to the question every one of your customers is privately asking — "why am I paying triple the UAE price?" — and you can answer it with named regulation instead of vibes. The stacked-duty mechanism plus minimum customs valuation (where duty is charged on a government-set value that can exceed what the importer actually paid) explains most of the gap. It also reframes the cheap listings: a seller at a third of the market price has either skipped the duty, parallel-imported, or is not selling the real thing. **Verification caveat for the writer:** the 2025-26 reduction reports conflicted and I could not confirm them against the current Finance Act. Cite the Act directly or mark the figures as of a stated date. Do not publish stale duty percentages as current.
