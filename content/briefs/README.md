# Content briefs — theeditpk.com

18 briefs: 3 clusters, each a pillar plus 5 supporting posts. Research run 2026-11-05.

**Nothing here is drafted.** Each file is a brief: primary and secondary keywords, intent, what currently ranks, the gap, a proposed title, an H2 outline, FAQs, and the product and collection URLs it should link to. Drafting happens from an approved brief, one at a time.

## No search volumes anywhere, by design

No keyword tool is connected to this project — no Search Console, no DataForSEO, no Ahrefs, no Semrush. There is not a single search volume, difficulty score or CPC figure in any of these briefs, and none should be added later by estimating.

Priorities are reasoned from what was actually observed: 18 web searches in English and Roman Urdu, roughly 40 Pakistani competitor page titles, and competitor URL patterns. The strongest signal used is competitor slugs that spell the query out — `qeemat.com.pk` builds URLs shaped `/dp/127044/medicube-zero-pore-one-day-cream-price-in-pakistan`, and `thevault.pk` uses the collection handle `cerave-skincare-products-price-in-pakistan`. A retailer only writes that if it believes people type it.

Once Search Console has data, these priorities should be re-ordered against real impressions.

## The clusters

**1 — Authenticity and honest pricing.** Six-plus Pakistani competitors independently converge on "original [brand] in Pakistan", and every one asserts authenticity without naming a supply chain. This store can name its own: licensed UAE pharmacies for the nine pharmacy brands, and a separate official-partner relationship for CHAM.

**2 — "Which product for my skin type".** The clearest question-shaped demand found. Maps one-to-one onto collections that already exist: `/collections/oily-acne` (29 products), `/collections/sensitive-skin` (37), `/collections/brightening` (19), `/collections/spf` (20).

**3 — Brand-vs-brand comparisons.** Not one Pakistani retailer has published a serious comparison, despite most stocking both brands. The global attempts found were content-farm pages on hijacked domains, which signals commercial pull with no quality incumbent.

## Publishing order

The binding constraint is domain trust, not keyword difficulty: the blog has zero posts and the store has no reviews or orders. So start where competition is thinnest, not where demand is highest.

1. **1.3 Avène** and **1.4 Dermalogica** — the clearest vacuums, 37 SKUs between them. A search for Avène Cicalfate returned Bangladeshi, Indian, UAE and Saudi listings and **no Pakistani price listing at all**. Dermalogica surfaced exactly one Pakistani seller and no distributor page.
2. **2.3 Sensitive skin** — the largest collection (37 products) against the thinnest concern-level competition.
3. **3.2 Avène vs La Roche-Posay** — comparison SERP with no credible incumbent.
4. **1.5 Import costs** and **3.5 Routine cost** — trust assets that answer the objections costing conversions now.
5. **2.2** and **3.4 Sunscreen** — best commercial value and repeat-purchase economics, but contested. Publish once the domain has traction.
6. **1.1 CeraVe** and **1.2 La Roche-Posay** — saturated. Worth doing for catalogue completeness, not early wins.

Publish each pillar *after* two or three of its supporting posts. Pillars target the broadest terms and rank last on a new domain. The internal links in each brief assume all 18 pages eventually exist — add them as pages go live rather than shipping dead links.

## Keywords deliberately excluded

Screened out before the clusters were built, not after. These have real demand but their intent requires a claim the store cannot make:

- `rang gora karne wali cream`, `best whitening cream in Pakistan`, `skin whitening serum`, `gora rang`, `fairness cream` — a searcher typing these wants a shade change. The SERP is dominated by hydroquinone and tretinoin products and by fairness creams at Rs 200–900. Ranking here buys bounces and refund requests.
- `melasma treatment Pakistan`, `melasma ka ilaj`, `keel muhase ka ilaj`, `daag door karne ka tarika`, `eczema cream`, `acne ka ilaj` — *ilaj* means treatment or cure. These are medical queries.

**Targeted instead:** `dark spots`, `uneven skin tone`, `brightening`, `post-acne marks`, `daag dhabbe` (descriptive — "spots and marks", not "cure"), `pigmentation`. These describe the concern without promising to treat it, and they match the `/collections/brightening` name already in use.

Framing rules that apply to every draft: Avène XeraCalm and Cicalfate are described as *for* very dry or eczema-prone and *for* compromised skin — never as treating or healing either.

## Open items before drafting

Verified during review:

- **Two product listings look like duplicates.** `/products/acm-sensitelial-emollient-care` and `/products/laboratoire-acm-sensitelial-emollient-care` are almost certainly the same product listed twice. `/products/depiwhite-advanced` and `/products/acm-depiwhite-anti-brown-spot-cream` likewise — the first one's live page title is "ACM Depiwhite Advanced Intensive Anti-Brown Spot Cream". Confirm in admin and remove one of each pair; otherwise any ACM page written here competes with a duplicate.
- **No Avène thermal water SKU exists.** The only thermal-water product in the sitemap is La Roche-Posay's. Brief 1.3 has an H2 on Avène's thermal spring water — confirm whether that SKU is stocked before writing it.
- **ACM has 16 handles, not 14.** Audit 1's brand counts are off by this much; the duplicates above explain it.
- **Two collections are too thin to be link targets.** `/collections/anti-aging` (3 products) and `/collections/night-treatment` (4). Briefs mostly avoid routing to them. Merge or grow them first.
- **A claim risk already live:** the handle `la-roche-posay-effaclar-duo-treatment-cream-for-acne-with-spf30-40ml-1` says "treatment cream for acne" in a URL the store controls.

Unverified, needs a human:

- **CHAM brand attribution.** `/products/brightening-face-mask` and `/products/the-brightening-cream` are inferred to be the CHAM products from the catalogue counts, not confirmed — the product pages returned HTTP 429. A CHAM search also surfaced an Indian "Cham Beauty" listing giving India as country of origin, which conflicts with designed-in-France/produced-in-China and may be an unrelated brand entirely. Confirm with the partner, not from search results.
- **Pakistani duty percentages.** SRO.928(I)/2024 and a 55% regulatory duty come from news reports; claimed 2025-26 reductions conflicted across sources. Verify against the current Finance Act before publishing brief 1.5, and date any figure used.
- **All competitor prices** in these briefs are from search-result snippets, undated and unverified on-site, some marked sold out. Re-check before publishing any price comparison.
- **Reddit.** r/PakistaniSkincare exists and is active but could not be opened. It is the best available source for real phrasing — worth a manual browse.
- **Roman Urdu demand** is inferred from SERP composition and one competitor's Roman Urdu post. Direction is sound; magnitude is unmeasured.
