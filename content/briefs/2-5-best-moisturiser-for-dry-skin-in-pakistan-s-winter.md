- **Intent:** Commercial, strongly seasonal. Publish by October for the Lahore/Islamabad/Peshawar winter.
- **What ranking pages appear to cover:** well populated but mass-market. thevault.pk (`which-moisturizer-is-best-for-dry-skin-in-pakistan`), snapcart.pk (*"Best Moisturiser for Dry Skin in Pakistan 2026 — Affordable to Premium"*), dnd.com.pk, chasevalue.pk, theskinfit.com (*"7 Winter Skincare Tips for Dry Weather in Pakistan"*), derma.pk (urea guide), nuvari.pk. CeraVe AM with SPF 30 appears occasionally. derma.pk's urea guide is the only ingredient-led piece I saw.
- **The gap you fill:** every competitor lists creams; nobody explains the *mechanism*, which is what lets a reader choose for themselves. Humectant / emollient / occlusive, why glycerin alone fails in dry winter air, why ceramides matter for a barrier that is losing water, and the application detail that changes outcomes more than the product does — apply to damp skin within a few minutes of washing. Then separate face from body properly: you hold 9 Aveeno SKUs, which is a genuine body-care range, and body dryness in a Punjabi winter is a real complaint nobody serves with dermo-cosmetic options. Also handle the case competitors ignore entirely: oily skin that goes dry and flaky in winter but still breaks out, which needs a gel-cream, not a rich balm.

# CLUSTER 3 — Brand-vs-brand comparisons and what a routine actually costs

**Why this cluster.** Comparison intent is almost entirely unserved *in Pakistan*. skinsort.com has hundreds of programmatic `compare/[product-a]-vs-[product-b]` pages and skinsignal.com has more, but they are ingredient tables with no narrative, no local prices and no availability. The global editorial attempts I found were content-farm pages on hijacked domains (`monitorbp.ne.gov/post/avene-vs-la-roche-posay`, `learn.omniafishing.com/?p=12091`, `2024.radiookapi.net/?p=42462`) — a tell that the keyword has commercial pull and no quality incumbent. **Not one Pakistani retailer has published a serious brand-vs-brand comparison**, despite most of them stocking both brands.

**Your advantage:** you stock all of them, so you can compare without a dog in the fight, and you can add the two dimensions every comparison page lacks — **PKR price** and **actually in stock in Pakistan**. Ingredient parity is easy to find; "which of these can I buy today and what will the routine cost me per month" is not findable anywhere.
