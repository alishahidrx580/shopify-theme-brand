## 3.5 What a three-step routine actually costs in Pakistan

- **Primary keyword:** skincare routine cost in Pakistan
- **Secondary:** affordable skincare routine Pakistan · skincare routine under 10000 Pakistan · basic skincare routine Pakistan price · cheap skincare routine Pakistan
- **Intent:** Commercial, budget-led. Catches readers who would otherwise bounce off your price point — and converts them by showing the maths rather than hiding it.
- **What ranking pages appear to cover:** the price-bracket framing is proven. nuvari.pk runs *"How to Build a Simple Skincare Routine from Scratch Under Rs. 3000 in Pakistan"*; malak.com.pk has a budget-routine guide; chasevalue.pk has the affordable-oily-skin version. All converge on cleanser + moisturiser + sunscreen, adding actives only after about four weeks, and advise 4–6 weeks before judging results. All build their brackets from mass-market products (sunscreen as low as Rs 349).
- **The gap you fill:** you will lose a straight Rs 3,000 race and should not enter it. Win by changing the unit from *price* to **cost per day**, which is the honest comparison and the one that favours you: a 473ml CeraVe cleanser and a 454g cream last months, so a routine that looks expensive on the receipt is often cheaper per use than repeatedly rebuying 100ml drugstore bottles. Publish three real tiers with live PKR totals and per-day maths, and — the detail competitors never give — **how long each product actually lasts** at correct usage, including the honest note that a 50ml sunscreen applied at the proper amount daily does not last a year. Also state plainly where cheap is fine and where it isn't: a basic cleanser is a commodity, sunscreen is not. That earns the trust to sell the sunscreen.

1. **1.3 Avène** and **1.4 Dermalogica** — the clearest vacuums I found, and 37 SKUs between them. Near-zero Pakistani competition means these can rank on a new domain.
